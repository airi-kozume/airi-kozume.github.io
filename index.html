About

I'm a technically curious tinkerer with a particular weakness for old computers, weird operating systems, and the parts of the internet most people have forgotten existed. I run my own infrastructure - FreeBSD servers, snac2, Caddy, an ATProto PDS - and enjoy digging into jails, ZFS, networking, PKI, and legacy protocols. Recent work includes porting an old HTTP proxy off deprecated OpenSSL onto wolfSSL.

I have a soft spot for the retro internet: CRTs, old Windows machines, classic consoles, archived sites, and obsolete protocols - the era when the internet felt like a collection of independently run places rather than a handful of giant platforms. I'm especially interested in the boundary between "obsolete" and "still perfectly usable if you know what you're doing."

Outside of infrastructure, I'm into games and visual novels with enough character to inspire an unreasonable amount of analysis - GameCube/Wii-era stuff, retro PC gaming, local multiplayer, and titles like Doki Doki Literature Club. I tend to turn favorite characters or media into little personal projects rather than just consuming them and moving on.

Privacy and ownership are recurring themes in most of what I do. I prefer software and infrastructure I can inspect, configure, host, repair, or replace myself, and I'd rather understand a system and work around its limits than accept an ecosystem's assumption that its way is the only legitimate one.
